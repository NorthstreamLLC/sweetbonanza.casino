Free Spins explained

Sweet Bonanza 1000 Bonus Guide

Triggers, retriggers, multiplier bombs and the difference between standard and Super Free Spins.

Quick answer: four or more lollipop scatters trigger ten Free Spins. Three or more scatters during the feature add five spins. Multiplier bombs are active in Free Spins and can reach 1,000×; multiple bomb values can be added before applying to a qualifying tumble win.

How to trigger Sweet Bonanza 1000 Free Spins

Free Spins begin when at least four lollipop scatter symbols land anywhere on the 6×5 grid. The scatter count also produces a separate scatter award under the game’s paytable. The regular trigger awards ten Free Spins.

The outcome is random. A near miss, a long base-game session or a previous bonus does not make the next spin “due.” If you are learning the trigger and controls, use the free demo guide where available.

How multiplier bombs work

During Free Spins, multiplier bombs can land with values from the feature’s permitted range. When a winning cluster forms, bomb values present in the applicable tumble sequence are added together and the combined multiplier is applied to the accumulated win.

For example, 10× and 25× bombs in the same qualifying sequence combine to 35×. That example explains the addition rule; it is not a prediction of how frequently those symbols appear. A 1,000× bomb is the maximum value of one bomb, not a guaranteed payout or the value of every feature.

Retriggers add five spins

Three or more lollipop scatters during Free Spins add five spins to the remaining total. Retriggers can extend the feature and create more opportunities for multiplier bombs, but they do not change the random nature of subsequent spins.

The game’s 25,000× maximum win cap still applies. Once the cap is reached, the round follows the stopping rules shown in the game information.

Super Free Spins remove lower multiplier-bomb values from the permitted range by raising the minimum bomb. That changes the multiplier floor, not the certainty of a profitable result. The feature can still return less than its entry cost.

Bonus Buy and Ante Bet

In supported markets, the feature menu can provide direct entry to standard or Super Free Spins. The commonly shown prices are 100× the selected stake for standard Free Spins and 500× for Super Free Spins. Confirm the price and rules displayed in your version before selecting anything.

Ante Bet is different from a feature purchase. It increases the paid-spin cost and changes scatter probability, but it does not guarantee a bonus. Neither option is a strategy for removing the house edge.

Availability warning: Bonus Buy, Ante Bet and demo access can vary by operator and jurisdiction. Do not use a VPN or another account to bypass a regional restriction.

What the 25,000× maximum means

The published maximum win is 25,000 times the stake. At a $0.20 stake, that ceiling equals $5,000; at $1, it equals $25,000. These calculations translate the multiplier and do not estimate the chance of reaching it.

Multiplier bombs and tumble wins must combine under the feature rules to produce the final payout. For broader context on the RTP, configuration variants and the original game, read the Sweet Bonanza 1000 RTP and features guide.
