How the theoretical return, tumble system, multiplier bombs and Free Spins fit together.

Sweet Bonanza 1000 RTP explained

RTP means return to player. It is a long-run mathematical percentage calculated across a very large number of rounds—not a promise that one session, bonus or player will receive that percentage back. A displayed RTP of 96.53% does not mean a $100 session should return $96.53.

Sweet Bonanza 1000 can be supplied in more than one mathematical configuration. The version reviewed across this site uses 96.53% as its standard figure, while lower configurations have also been documented. Because an operator can select a permitted configuration, the reliable place to confirm your version is the information or help panel inside the game.

How tumble wins work

Sweet Bonanza 1000 does not use fixed paylines. A win forms when enough matching symbols land anywhere on the grid. Winning symbols disappear, new symbols tumble into the open spaces, and another win can form without placing a new wager. This sequence continues until a tumble produces no new win.

The tumble mechanic matters because several wins can be created from one paid spin. It does not guarantee that a sequence will continue, and a longer tumble animation is not evidence that the next spin is more likely to win.

Multiplier bombs: 2× to 1,000×

Multiplier bombs are active during Free Spins. When at least one winning cluster forms, the values on multiplier bombs present in that tumble sequence are added together and applied to the accumulated win. The sequel expands the top individual bomb value to 1,000×.

A 1,000× symbol is the ceiling of one multiplier bomb, not the expected value of a bonus. Most rounds will not reach the published maximum, and multiple multipliers only matter when the feature rules allow them to be applied to a qualifying win.

Free Spins and retriggers

Four or more lollipop scatter symbols trigger the main Free Spins feature. The game begins with ten Free Spins, and three or more scatters during the feature add five more. Multiplier bombs are the key difference between base play and the bonus round.

Sweet Bonanza 1000 also includes a Super Free Spins version in supported markets. Its multiplier bombs have a higher minimum value, changing the feature’s multiplier range. The entry method and purchase options can vary by jurisdiction, so the controls displayed in one demo should not be assumed to exist everywhere.

Ante Bet and Bonus Buy

Ante Bet raises the stake in exchange for a changed scatter setup intended to increase the chance of triggering the feature. It does not guarantee Free Spins. A Bonus Buy, where permitted, provides direct feature entry for the price shown in the game.

These are paid configuration choices, not strategies that overcome the house edge. Before selecting either option, confirm the total cost, the RTP attached to that mode and any local restrictions.
