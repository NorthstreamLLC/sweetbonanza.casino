What is Sweet Bonanza Super Scatter?

Sweet Bonanza Super Scatter is a special edition of Pragmatic Play's original candy-themed slot. It keeps the 6×5 pays-anywhere grid, tumbles, ordinary lollipop Scatters and Free Spins multipliers, then adds a new Super Scatter symbol to the bonus trigger.

The core game still pays when eight or more matching fruit or candy symbols appear anywhere on the screen. A winning group disappears, new symbols fall into the gaps and another win can form during the same paid spin. The Super Scatter does not replace that system; it adds an instant-prize layer when the bonus is triggered.

How the regular bonus works

Without a Super Scatter, four to six ordinary lollipops still award ten Free Spins and an ordinary Scatter payout from 3× to 100×. During Free Spins, candy-bomb multiplier symbols can land with values up to 100×.

Multipliers stay visible until the current tumble sequence finishes. Their displayed values are then added together and applied to the total tumble win. Three or more ordinary Scatters during the feature add five more spins. A multiplier on screen is not a payout by itself; a qualifying symbol win is still required.

Important: a 50,000× ceiling does not mean the feature commonly pays 50,000×. The maximum requires the published four-Super-Scatter trigger, and its probability is not disclosed on the public provider pages reviewed.

RTP, volatility and unavailable statistics

The public Pragmatic Play game and launch pages reviewed for this guide do not display an RTP percentage, numerical volatility rating, hit frequency or maximum-win probability. We do not copy those figures from other affiliate sites. Open the information screen in the exact game version you are offered and use the operator-displayed rules.

RTP, when displayed, is a long-run theoretical value and cannot predict one session. Previous tumbles, demo results and streamer clips do not make a Super Scatter more likely on the next spin.

Our independent review

Super Scatter makes a clear change without rewriting the familiar game. The instant-prize ladder is easy to understand, and the four-symbol 50,000× award gives this edition a distinct reason to exist alongside the 1000 and 2500 sequels.

The trade-off is that the most marketable outcome is tied to a rare-event mechanism whose public probability is not supplied. Players should distinguish the exciting ceiling from a typical result and should not increase stakes to chase a particular Scatter count.
