Sweet Bonanza 2500 Bonus Guide

How the standard bonus and the published Super Free Spins options differ—and why availability must be checked locally.

Standard Free Spins

Landing four or more lollipop scatters in the base game triggers 10 free spins. During the feature, one or more candy-bomb multipliers can land. Each bomb remains for the active tumble chain. When that chain ends, the bomb values add together and multiply the tumble win.

Three or more lollipops landing during the bonus add five more free spins. Pragmatic Play's public page describes this as repeatable whenever the condition is met; it does not publish an average retrigger frequency.

The 2,500× multiplier

A candy bomb can reach 2,500×, but the number is a maximum—not a minimum or expected result. Multiple bombs add rather than multiply together. The game's total published win cap is 25,000×, so play ends or awards are capped according to the provider's rules once that limit is reached.

Super Free Spins options

Higher-minimum mode

Pragmatic Play says the first Super Free Spins option can raise the minimum multiplier value up to 20×.

Progressive-total mode

The second begins with a 10× minimum and activates a total multiplier that increases through the feature, affecting later wins.

These are distinct choices, not guarantees of profit. Their price, RTP and presence depend on the operator version and local market rules.

Special bets and Bonus Buy

The provider also publishes special-bet and Bonus Buy options that can unlock base-game multipliers, higher guaranteed minimum multipliers, or direct Super Free Spins access. Because the launch announcement does not provide one universal price sheet, this guide does not invent fixed costs. Read the purchase confirmation and in-game help before selecting an option.

Responsible-play check: direct feature purchases concentrate more money into fewer rounds. Decide a total loss limit first; do not increase the next purchase to recover the previous one.

Bonus checklist

  1. Confirm the displayed stake and total purchase cost.
  2. Open the help screen and record the RTP for the selected mode.
  3. Check whether the mode guarantees only entry, or also changes minimum multipliers.
  4. Remember that 2,500× is the largest single published bomb—not a promised bonus return.
  5. Stop at the pre-set money or time limit.
