Sweet Bonanza 2500 vs Sweet Bonanza 1000

Both publish a 25,000× maximum win and use the same familiar trigger structure. Sweet Bonanza 2500 raises the largest individual bomb from 1,000× to 2,500× and introduces a broader set of special bets and Super Free Spins choices in eligible markets. The key distinction is therefore how the feature can build toward the ceiling, not a higher total ceiling.

Sweet Bonanza 2500 vs the original

The original's largest bonus multiplier is 100× and its launch announcement publishes a 21,175× maximum win. Sweet Bonanza 2500 pushes the individual multiplier to 2,500× and the total ceiling to 25,000×. The trade-off is complexity: the original presents the cleanest version of the feature, whereas 2500 asks players to evaluate more optional modes.

Which one should you play?

Choose the original for simplicity

It is the clearest introduction to pays-anywhere wins, tumbles and free-spin multipliers.

Choose 1000 for the middle ground

It lifts the multiplier and max-win ceilings without the 2500 feature menu.

Choose 2500 for feature variety

It offers the largest single bomb and the widest published selection of optional bonus paths.

No version is “due” to pay, and a larger maximum does not mean larger average session results. Check the exact RTP in each game's help screen before comparing mathematical return.

Comparison FAQs

Does 2,500× mean Sweet Bonanza 2500 pays more often?
No. It is a maximum multiplier value, not a hit-frequency statistic. The provider's public page does not publish a comparative frequency.
Why do 1000 and 2500 share the same max win?
The largest individual multiplier rises, but Pragmatic Play publishes the same 25,000× total win cap for both games.
