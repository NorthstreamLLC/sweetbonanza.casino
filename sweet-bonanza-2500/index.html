What changed in Sweet Bonanza 2500?

The core remains familiar: matching symbols pay anywhere on a 6×5 grid, winning symbols tumble away, and new symbols fall into the empty spaces. The headline change is a new 2,500× maximum candy-bomb multiplier in the bonus game. That is 2.5 times the multiplier ceiling published for Sweet Bonanza 1000 and 25 times the original game's 100× ceiling.

The total published maximum win remains 25,000× the stake. The larger single multiplier therefore changes how that ceiling can be reached; it does not raise the ceiling beyond Sweet Bonanza 1000.

Core rules at a glance

  • Wins: 8–12 or more matching fruit or candy symbols landing anywhere on the grid.
  • Tumbles: symbols in a winning combination disappear, allowing another result within the same paid spin.
  • Bonus trigger: four or more lollipops award 10 free spins.
  • Multiplier bombs: bonus multipliers remain through a tumble sequence, add together and apply to the total tumble win.
  • Retrigger: three or more lollipops during the bonus add five free spins.

Expanded options

Pragmatic Play says special bets and Bonus Buy choices are available in select markets. Depending on the operator and jurisdiction, these can unlock base-game multipliers, increase guaranteed minimum multipliers, or open Super Free Spins. Availability and cost must be checked inside the version offered by the casino.

Frequently asked questions

Is Sweet Bonanza 2500 a sequel?
Yes. It retains the series' 6×5 pays-anywhere and tumble format while increasing the maximum bonus multiplier to 2,500× and adding more optional feature modes.
Does it pay 25,000×?
25,000× is the provider-published maximum win, not an expected result. Actual results are random and most sessions will not approach the maximum.
Is the Bonus Buy available everywhere?
No. Pragmatic Play describes these options as available in select markets. Operator and local rules determine what appears.
