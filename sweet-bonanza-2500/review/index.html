Sweet Bonanza 2500 Review

A bigger multiplier ceiling and broader bonus menu make this a meaningful mechanical update, though the 25,000× total win ceiling does not increase over Sweet Bonanza 1000.

Our verdict

Sweet Bonanza 2500 is the version for players who want the familiar grid but more ways to alter the feature. The 2,500× bomb is a clear upgrade over 1000; the more important design change may be the menu of special bets and two Super Free Spins formats. Players who prefer the simplest version may find the original easier to read.

Strengths

  • Series-high 2,500× bonus multiplier
  • Clear, familiar tumble mechanics
  • Multiple feature paths in eligible markets
  • Provider-published 25,000× maximum win

Trade-offs

  • 25,000× ceiling is unchanged from SB 1000
  • Special options are not available everywhere
  • More modes make cost and RTP checking essential
  • High headline multipliers do not imply frequent wins

Gameplay and presentation

The base game deliberately avoids reinventing Sweet Bonanza. Eight or more matching treats pay anywhere and winning symbols tumble. This makes the game immediately readable for returning players. The provider publishes base-game symbol wins of up to 50×, while the larger headline outcomes depend on feature multipliers.

The bonus still begins with four lollipops and 10 spins. Multiplier bombs stay on the grid for the current tumble chain, combine, and apply to its total win. A single bomb can now reach 2,500×. Retriggers still add five spins when three or more lollipops land.

Feature depth

In select markets, optional modes can bring multipliers into the base game, raise minimum bonus multipliers or trigger Super Free Spins. Pragmatic Play describes one Super Free Spins option with a minimum multiplier that can reach 20×. A second starts at a 10× minimum and adds a progressive total multiplier that grows through the feature.

Those options improve variety but require careful reading: availability, price and the displayed RTP can vary by operator configuration. The safest approach is to compare the exact in-game help screen, not assume every casino offers the same version.

Who should choose it?

Choose Sweet Bonanza 2500 if the largest individual bomb and more configurable features matter to you. Consider Sweet Bonanza 1000 if you want the same 25,000× published ceiling with a simpler bonus format. The original remains the most straightforward series entry. None of these differences creates a reliable short-session advantage.
