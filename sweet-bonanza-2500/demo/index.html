What the free demo is useful for

A demo is best treated as an interface tutorial. It lets you see how pays-anywhere wins form, how tumbles can continue after a win, and how lollipop scatters open the bonus. It can also make the 2,500× multiplier headline easier to understand: bombs appear during the bonus, remain through the current tumble chain, add together and then apply to that chain's win.

Demo limitation: a short run of virtual-credit results cannot establish the game's RTP, hit rate or likely result of a future real-money session.

Five things to test

  1. Confirm that eight or more matching symbols can pay anywhere rather than on fixed paylines.
  2. Watch winning symbols disappear and new symbols tumble into the same spin.
  3. See that four or more lollipops trigger 10 free spins.
  4. During free spins, watch multipliers remain for the tumble sequence and combine.
  5. Look for three or more lollipops during the feature, which add five spins.

Before switching to real money

  • Open the in-game help or paytable and check the displayed RTP for that exact version.
  • Confirm whether special bets or Bonus Buy options are legal and enabled in your market.
  • Set a fixed entertainment budget and time limit before starting.
  • Do not use the demo balance or a demo win as a bankroll target.
