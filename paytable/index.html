Sweet Bonanza Paytable

Provider-published payout limits, pays-anywhere rules, and how to check exact symbol values.

How Pays-Anywhere Wins Work

Sweet Bonanza pays when 8 or more matching symbols appear anywhere on its 6×5 grid. The symbols do not need to touch or form a line. Winning symbols tumble away and new symbols fall into their places.

Check the Exact Symbol Values

Open the information or help panel in your game and select its paytable. Check the payout for the symbol and matching-symbol count, and whether values are shown as money, credits or stake multipliers. Do not read a credit value as a multiple of your total stake.

For example, a value explicitly labeled 0.5× total stake returns $0.50 on a $1 stake. The multiplier is a payout calculation, not a prediction of how often the win will occur.

Multiplier Bombs (Free Spins Only)

During the Free Spins round, multiplier symbols can appear. When bombs land and a tumble sequence ends, all visible multipliers are summed then applied to the entire win. They do not multiply each other — they add.

Pragmatic Play publishes a 2×–100× range for individual multiplier symbols. Its public page does not state how frequently each value appears.

Example: 25× + 50× + 100× combine to a 175× multiplier. This illustrates addition only; it does not predict the result of a spin.

Paytable FAQs

What symbol payout does the provider publish?
Check the symbol ranking and count bands in the in-game paytable. The provider’s public page states that matching-symbol awards reach up to 50× the stake.
How many symbols for a win?
A minimum of 8 identical symbols anywhere on the grid forms a win. Symbols don't need to be adjacent in a line — they just need to be the same type.
Is there a wild symbol?
No traditional wild. The lollipop is the scatter. There are no wild substitutions — wins come purely from matching symbols anywhere on the grid.
Do multiplier bombs stack or multiply?
They add, not multiply. Two 100× bombs in the same tumble = 200× total. This is an important distinction — many players assume they multiply.
Can I win from multiple symbol types in one tumble?
Yes. Multiple different symbol types can win simultaneously. If you land 9 grapes, 8 watermelons, and 10 plums all at once, all three pay out in the same tumble.
