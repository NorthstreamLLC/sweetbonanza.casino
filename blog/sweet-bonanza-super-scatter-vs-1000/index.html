Sweet Bonanza Super Scatter vs Sweet Bonanza 1000

Instant 50,000× Scatter prizes versus 1,000× multiplier bombs.

Sweet Bonanza 1000 and Sweet Bonanza Super Scatter share the candy theme, 6×5 Pays Anywhere grid and 10-spin bonus. Their headline mechanics are completely different. SB1000 raises the multiplier-bomb ceiling; Super Scatter adds rare feature-triggering symbols that can award an instant prize and keeps multiplier symbols on the grid during Free Spins.

How Sweet Bonanza 1000 Builds Wins

SB1000's central upgrade is the candy-bomb multiplier. During Free Spins, bombs can carry values up to 1,000×. The values present on the applicable tumbling sequence are added and applied to the win. The published maximum win is 25,000× the bet.

How Super Scatter Changes the Feature

Sweet Bonanza Super Scatter adds a second kind of trigger symbol. If one, two, three or four Super Scatters are part of the combination that activates Free Spins, the game awards an instant 100×, 500×, 5,000× or 50,000× prize.

During the feature, multiplier symbols reach up to 100× and remain on the grid until the feature finishes. At the end of a winning tumble, the visible multiplier values are added and applied to the win. The persistent-grid behavior, rather than one enormous individual bomb, is the defining mechanic.

The 50,000× Headline Needs Context

The largest published Super Scatter prize is also the game's 50,000× maximum win. It requires four Super Scatters in the feature-triggering combination. That ceiling describes a possible result, not an average result or a reason to increase a stake.

SB1000 has the lower 25,000× cap but the larger individual multiplier symbol. Comparing only 50,000× versus 25,000× misses how differently those outcomes are constructed.

The Bottom Line

Sweet Bonanza 1000 is the multiplier-bomb sequel. Sweet Bonanza Super Scatter is the instant-trigger-prize and persistent-multiplier version. Neither format guarantees more frequent wins. Use the demos to understand the mechanics, confirm the RTP inside the loaded game and keep the same limit whichever version you choose.

Comparison FAQs

Which game has the higher maximum win?
Sweet Bonanza Super Scatter at 50,000×. Sweet Bonanza 1000 has a 25,000× maximum.
What do Super Scatters pay?
One, two, three or four Super Scatters in the feature-triggering combination award 100×, 500×, 5,000× or 50,000× respectively.
Which game has the larger multiplier bomb?
SB1000 reaches 1,000×. Super Scatter uses multipliers up to 100× that can remain on the grid through the bonus.
Do both games have 10 Free Spins?
Yes, with five more spins awarded when three or more additional Scatters land during the feature.
