Sweet Bonanza 2500 vs Sweet Bonanza 1000

Same 25,000× win cap, different multiplier and feature paths.

Sweet Bonanza 2500 is not simply Sweet Bonanza 1000 with a larger number on the title. Both games keep Pragmatic Play's 6×5 Pays Anywhere grid, tumbles and 10-spin bonus, but the 2500 edition expands how multipliers can appear and how stronger Free Spins modes can be entered.

What Stays the Same

Both versions award wins when eight or more matching fruit or candy symbols land anywhere on the grid. A win tumbles away, new symbols fall in, and another qualifying cluster can pay without a new wager. Four lollipop Scatters start 10 Free Spins, while three or more during the feature add five more.

Both games also share a published maximum win of 25,000× the bet. The 2500 title therefore describes the largest individual multiplier symbol, not the total win cap.

The 2,500× Difference

Sweet Bonanza 1000 raises the classic game's maximum bomb to 1,000×. Sweet Bonanza 2500 increases that single-symbol ceiling again to 2,500×. Multiple bomb values landing in one applicable tumble are added before the total is applied to the win.

A higher ceiling expands the range of possible multiplier combinations. It does not tell you how often a 2,500× bomb appears, and it does not make a short session more likely to finish ahead.

Where Sweet Bonanza 2500 Adds More Choice

The 2500 edition's most meaningful change is its feature menu. In supported markets, special bets can introduce multipliers in the base game, raise guaranteed minimum multiplier values or open stronger Free Spins formats.

One Super Free Spins option raises the minimum multiplier to 20×. Another uses a 10× minimum and a progressive total that grows through the feature and boosts later wins. Availability and prices can vary, so the panel inside the loaded game is the only reliable operator-specific source.

The Bottom Line

Sweet Bonanza 1000 and 2500 share the same headline maximum win. The 2500 edition is the feature-rich choice; the 1000 edition is the cleaner upgrade from the classic game. Try both with virtual credits, read the in-game RTP and feature panel, and choose by mechanics rather than by a promise of better results.

Comparison FAQs

What is the main difference between Sweet Bonanza 2500 and 1000?
SB2500 raises the maximum bomb from 1,000× to 2,500× and adds more optional special-bet and Super Free Spins paths.
Which game has the higher maximum win?
Neither. Pragmatic Play publishes a 25,000× maximum win for both games.
Do both games award 10 Free Spins?
Yes. Four or more lollipop Scatters trigger 10 spins, and three or more Scatters during the feature add five.
Does the 2,500× multiplier make SB2500 more likely to win?
No. It is a higher theoretical multiplier-symbol ceiling, not a claim about hit frequency or short-term returns.
