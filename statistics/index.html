Statistics FAQs

What is the hit frequency of Sweet Bonanza?
Pragmatic Play's public Sweet Bonanza page does not publish an official hit-frequency percentage. Treat precise figures on third-party sites as estimates unless they cite a verifiable source.
How often does the bonus trigger?
Four or more lollipop scatters trigger 10 Free Spins, but the provider's public page does not state an average number of spins between triggers.
What % of RTP comes from Free Spins?
The provider does not publicly break the total RTP into base-game and Free Spins shares.
Is the RTP 96.49% guaranteed in every session?
No. 96.49% is a theoretical long-run figure, not a session guarantee. Pragmatic Play describes Sweet Bonanza as high volatility, so short-session results can vary sharply.
What bankroll do you need?
There is no bankroll that guarantees a bonus or protects against loss. Set a firm spending and time limit, use only disposable entertainment money, and never chase losses.
